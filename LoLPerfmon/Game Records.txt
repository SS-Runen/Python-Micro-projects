Role Ratio Tracking
One role is a priority, one is a secondary, and any other roles are low-priority.
Low-priority roles will be assigned one game to every four of the priority role and two of the secondary.
Low-priority roles will be played in a round-robin style and can be arbitrarily omitted.
If a role is played the number of required games/times, it will be marked with a pipe (|) or other delimiter symbol.
